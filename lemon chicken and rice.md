One pot lemon chicken and rice. Goes really well with tzatziki sauce.

Ingredients:

- Oil
- One onion (chopped)
- Ginger (optional)
- Cinnamon
- Turmeric
- Salt and pepper
- 1/3 cup lemon juice
- Tomato paste
- Chicken (cut into pieces)
- Rice (washed and strained)
- Broth or water

Steps:
1. Fry the onion in oil until soft and golden
1. Add the ginger if using, then the cinnamon, turmeric, salt and pepper, and stir for a minute
1. Add the lemon juice and tomato paste and mix until the paste dissolves
1. Add the chicken and cook, stirring, until it's browned on the outside
1. Add the rice and stir to coat it in the sauce
1. Add broth or water (about 1.5 to 2 cups per cup of rice) and bring to a boil
1. Cover, turn the heat to low, and cook until the rice is done and the liquid is absorbed (about 20 minutes)
1. Let it rest covered for 5 to 10 minutes, then fluff and serve with tzatziki
